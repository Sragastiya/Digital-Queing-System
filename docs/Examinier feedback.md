Examiner feedback
1. Update wait estimates automatically. Adjust the estimate as actual wait times and queue conditions change.
2. Account for group size. Estimate the wait using the number of people ahead in the queue instead of treating every ticket equally.
3. Define what happens when a group cannot be seated. Decide how staff should handle the next group if the available tables cannot fit everyone.
4. Allow temporary service suspension. Let staff stop issuing new tickets during disruptions, such as supply shortages or severe weather, and cancel pending tickets if needed.
5. Validate the design with real users. If possible, interview restaurant staff and customers to understand how they actually manage and use the queue.
6. Separate dine-in and takeaway queues. Staff should be able to serve takeaway customers when service capacity allows, even if all tables are occupied.
Presentation note: Keep the theory brief and focus on how the system handles real situations.
